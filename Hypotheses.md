# Hypotheses – Study 2

### H1
Higher levels of interviewee resistance lead to lower interviewer-rated
**reliability**, independent of scenario severity.

### H2
Interviewers’ ratings of **cooperation** are affected by both resistance level
and scenario severity, with comparable effect sizes.

### H3
Higher scenario severity leads to lower interviewer-rated **loyalty** and
higher perceived **vulnerability**, independent of resistance level.

### H4
The negative effect of resistance on **reliability** is stronger under
high scenario severity than under low severity.

### H5
At higher resistance levels, interviewers will perceive the interviewee as
less cooperative than the interviewee reports themselves
(ΔRBI becomes more negative).

### H6
There is substantial **between-interviewer variability** in judgments
(random intercept variance > 0), even when resistance and scenario are held constant.

### H7
Interviewers are more likely to respond “Not fully cooperative” at higher
resistance levels, with sensitivity moderated by scenario severity.
