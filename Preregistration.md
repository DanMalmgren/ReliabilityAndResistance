# Preregistration – Study 2

## Study overview

This study examines how **interviewee resistance** and **scenario severity**
jointly influence interviewers’ judgments in a simulated security interview.

University students are randomly assigned to the role of **interviewer (INT)**
or **interviewee (DET)**. Interviewees enact a predefined role that combines
a fixed **scenario** (type of substance) and a fixed **resistance level**.
Interviewers may conduct multiple interviews.

After each interview, both parties complete structured questionnaires.

---

## Experimental design

### Factors

**Resistance (manipulated, between interviewees, within interviewers)**
- Open / cooperative  
- Selective / avoidant  
- Defensive  

**Scenario severity (manipulated, between interviewees, within interviewers)**
- Marijuana  
- Sedatives  
- Cocaine  

Each interviewee (DET) is assigned **one fixed combination** of resistance × scenario
and enacts the same role in all interviews.

Interviewers (INT) conduct multiple interviews and are exposed to all
resistance × scenario combinations via balanced block randomization.

---

## Measures

### Interviewer-rated outcomes

#### Engagement (ordinal, 1–5)
Single item.

#### Cooperation
- Binary: Cooperated (Yes / No)
- Ordinal: Cooperation level (1–5)

#### Likert-based judgments (1–7)

Items are grouped into three constructs:

**Reliability**
- Likert4 (Reliable)
- Likert8 (Risk of unreliability) – *reverse coded*
- Likert9 (Follows laws and rules)
- Likert13 (Laws and rules not a hindrance)

**Loyalty**
- Likert3 (Assumed loyalty)
- Likert6 (Risk of loyalty conflict) – *reverse coded*
- Likert7 (Values loyalty to Sweden higher)

**Vulnerability**
- Likert1 (Alcohol not a hindrance) – *reverse coded*
- Likert2 (Travel not a hindrance) – *reverse coded*
- Likert5 (No vulnerability hindering placement) – *reverse coded*
- Likert10 (Social network not a vulnerability) – *reverse coded*
- Likert11 (Risk of future vulnerability)
- Likert12 (Drugs not a hindrance) – *reverse coded*

After coding, higher scores indicate:
- higher **reliability**
- higher **loyalty**
- higher **vulnerability**

---

### Interviewee self-ratings

- Engagement (1–5)
- Cooperation (1–5)
- Manipulation checks:
  - Scenario type
  - Resistance level enacted

---

## Exclusion criteria (predefined)

- Failed manipulation check for scenario or resistance
- Missing outcome data for primary analyses

No exclusions based on model fit or statistical outcomes.
